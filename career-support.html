More Than a Job Platform

Technical Talent Should Also Change the Systems It Joins

The Talent Hub connects organisations with women who bring both strong technical capabilities and an understanding of the social and political questions surrounding technology — across AI, ML, data science, NLP, generative AI, policy and public-interest technology.

  • Artificial intelligence, machine learning & data science
  • Responsible and feminist AI, digital rights & policy
  • Research, product design & civic technology
